## Our wedding vows

### We vow to protect our love.

We vow to protect our love from selfishness, considering each other’s needs before our own.

We vow to protect our love through patience, never allowing life’s daily challenges to interrupt our caring for one another.

We vow to protect our love from doubt, never violating each other’s trust.

We vow to protect our love from complacency, remembering our love is precious and requires our attention and effort.

### We vow to nurture our love.

We vow to nurture our love through communication, both hearing and listening, never concealing our thoughts, so that we may know each other’s needs and desires.

We vow to nurture our love with empathy, taking each other’s needs and desires as our own, providing solutions when we can, and understanding when we can’t.

We vow to nurture our love by sharing our ambitions, goals, and paths so that we may walk through life together.

We vow to nurture our love by caring for our own selves, being our best, so that we may be worthy of the love we are given.

### We vow to demonstrate our love.

We vow to demonstrate our love for each other through affection knowing just a touch of the hand reaffirms our commitment.

We vow to demonstrate our love through mutual respect making it known that we value each other above all else.

We vow to never cease being sweethearts.
